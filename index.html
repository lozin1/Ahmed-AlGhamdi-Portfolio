About Me

I'm Ahmed Rami AlGhamdi, a developer and student at Gifted Students Technical High School (الموهوبين التقنية الثانوية) in Jeddah, Saudi Arabia. I specialise in Python and C, with a strong focus on machine learning, data analysis, and building software that solves real-world problems.

I'm part of the Tuwaiq Academy ecosystem — a Ministry of Education programme that connects gifted students with industry. I'm also affiliated with Mawhiba (King Abdulaziz Foundation for Giftedness & Creativity), which pushes me beyond conventional learning and toward industry-level challenges.

I believe in shipping, learning, and iterating — building real solutions to real-world problems.
